Information Security Policy

Acme Trading LLC — Version 3.0 — Effective 1 March 2026

1. Purpose

This policy protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of Acme's information. It applies to all employees, contractors and systems that store or process company data.

2. Data classification

  • Public: approved for release outside the company.
  • Internal: the default for business documents; share only with employees.
  • Confidential: contracts, pricing and personal data; share on a need-to-know basis and encrypt in transit.
  • Restricted: board papers and credentials; access must be approved by the data owner and reviewed every quarter.

3. Passwords and access

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, VPN and every system holding Confidential or Restricted data. Access is removed within one business day when an employee leaves or changes role.

4. Incident reporting

Report suspected security incidents to the security team within 2 hours of discovery at security@acme.example. Do not try to investigate or contain an incident on your own.

5. Vendors

Vendors who will access Confidential data must sign a data processing agreement and pass a security review before the contract is approved under the Procurement Policy.
